THE SYSTEM

One profile. A connected set of decisions.

From the evidence in a CV to the actions that change your career, every recommendation should have a reason, a source, and a next step.

A NON-NEGOTIABLE PRINCIPLE

Can do ≠ may do.

Career data is personal. External sources can be untrusted. Recommendations can be wrong. A trustworthy product preserves provenance, respects permissions, and asks before taking consequential action.

Traceability

Claims link to evidence or are marked unverified.

Freshness

Market findings carry retrieval dates and expire.

Agency

Users can review, edit, approve, export, or delete information.
